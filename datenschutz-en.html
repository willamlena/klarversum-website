Protecting your data matters to me. Here you can find out which data is processed when you visit this website and during tutoring, and what rights you have. This is based on the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) and the Austrian Data Protection Act (DSG).

This English version is provided for convenience. The German version is legally binding.

1. Controller

Ramon Hämmerle, Klarversum – Nachhilfe (tutoring)
Reichsstraße 23, 6890 Lustenau, Austria
Email: klarversum@gmail.com

2. Hosting and server log files

This website is provided via GitHub Pages, a service of GitHub Inc., 88 Colin P. Kelly Jr. Street, San Francisco, CA 94107, USA. When you visit the site, technically necessary data such as your IP address, date and time, the page accessed and your browser type are processed in order to deliver the website and keep it secure (legitimate interest, Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR). This may involve transferring data to the USA. GitHub is certified under the EU-US Data Privacy Framework, for which the European Commission has found an adequate level of data protection (Art. 45 GDPR). More information: GitHub’s privacy statement.

3. Contact form

If you use the contact form, your name, email address, subject, school level and message are processed in order to answer your enquiry and arrange a trial lesson (pre-contractual measures, Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR). The form is sent via the Web3Forms service, which forwards your enquiry to me by email. The data is deleted once the enquiry has been dealt with and no tutoring has been agreed.

4. Contact by email and WhatsApp

For email, I use Gmail (Google Ireland Ltd.). If you contact me via WhatsApp, your data is also processed by WhatsApp Ireland Ltd. (Meta), and their privacy policy applies. The WhatsApp link on this website only establishes a connection once you click it.

5. Data processed during tutoring

To provide tutoring, I process the names and contact details of students and parents, school level, learning progress, appointments, lesson notes and billing data (performance of a contract, Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR). Online lessons take place via Google Meet, notes and whiteboard via Microsoft OneNote. Invoices and accounting records are kept for seven years in line with statutory retention obligations (§ 132 BAO, Austrian Federal Fiscal Code). All other data is deleted after tutoring ends, unless there is a legal obligation to keep it.

6. Fonts

The fonts used on this website are delivered directly with the website. When you visit the site, no connection is made to servers of Google or any other font provider.

7. Cookies and analytics

This website does not use cookies or any analytics or tracking services.

8. Your rights

You have the right at any time to access, rectify, erase and restrict the processing of your data, to data portability and to object. Simply write to klarversum@gmail.com.

If you believe that the processing of your data violates data protection law, you can lodge a complaint with the supervisory authority: Austrian Data Protection Authority (Datenschutzbehörde), Barichgasse 40–42, 1030 Vienna, dsb.gv.at.
