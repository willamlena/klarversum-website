About me · Ramon Hämmerle

May I introduce myself?

So glad you found your way here! Let me tell you a little about who and what is behind Klarversum.

After technical college (HTL) in electrical engineering and a few years working in development, I moved to Innsbruck in 2020 to study physics. Alongside that, I took courses in mathematics and teacher training and taught online for a tutoring organisation.

Because I really enjoy passing on what I know and teaching others, I became self-employed in 2026 after finishing my degree. That’s how Klarversum came to be.

I know the feeling of sitting in front of a problem without a clue where to start. That can be really frustrating, and it quickly takes the fun out of learning. Maths, physics, chemistry and electrical engineering in particular build on each other. If a foundation is missing somewhere, things get hard fast. Together we find the gaps and rebuild the missing basics. Often all it takes is an everyday example or a different perspective for it to finally click.

I deliberately teach one-to-one only, because everyone learns differently. In a relaxed, easy-going atmosphere we adapt the lessons to your pace until you understand the material and can apply it on your own. You can ask any question, even if it’s the same one for the third time! And if questions come up between lessons, I’m happy to help on WhatsApp.

The best part for me is when frustration turns back into motivation, when my students enjoy learning again and proudly tell me about their results after an exam.

That’s exactly what Klarversum stands for: learning together, closing gaps and seeing clearly again, in a relaxed atmosphere.

How it works

Here’s how it works.

  1. 01

    Getting to know each other

    We talk for 30 minutes, completely free.
    Where are you stuck, what’s the goal?
    What are your wishes?
    And most importantly: is the chemistry right between us?

  2. 02

    Learning plan

    Together we create a learning plan tailored to your lessons and your goals. With suitable worksheets and targeted exam preparation, we close gaps and get you ready for school tests and exams.

  3. 03

    Online lesson

    We meet via video call and work together on a digital whiteboard, just like at a blackboard. I explain how things connect, and we practise together until you understand the material and can apply it on your own.

  4. 04

    Feedback

    After every lesson, you get our whiteboard notes as a PDF and, if you like, matching worksheets or practice tests. Plus short feedback, so that you and your parents always stay up to date on your progress.

FAQ

Frequently asked questions.

Anything still unclear? Just message me.

Does online tutoring work as well as in person?

In fact, my experience is that online tutoring often works even better! 😊 Of course, it also depends on how you learn. On the digital whiteboard, we work together as if we were standing right in front of a blackboard. Plus, you save the journey there and back and can use that time for practising or revising.

Which programs do we use?

We meet via Google Meet. You don’t need your own account, and on a computer you don’t need any extra software either. I’ll send you the link before the lesson via WhatsApp or email. We work together on a digital whiteboard in OneNote. After every lesson, you get our notes as a PDF, so you can look everything up at any time and use it for studying.

How often should you take lessons?

That of course depends on how much material we need to catch up on. Generally, I recommend one lesson per week to close knowledge gaps and keep up with the current topics. That way, what you’ve learned sticks better and we avoid unnecessary stress before exams. If needed, we can of course add extra lessons before tests or exams.

How flexible is scheduling?

Very flexible! As I’m self-employed, I’m not tied to fixed hours. Weekends have proven to work especially well: there’s no school, everyone is more relaxed and minds are fresh. On weekday evenings, after a long day at school, concentration is often already used up. In the end, it’s your call. Just message me any time, we’ll always find a solution that works.

What happens if a lesson has to be cancelled?

No problem, it happens! You can cancel or reschedule free of charge up to 24 hours in advance. For later cancellations, I charge 50 % of the cost, and 100 % for lessons that are forgotten or not cancelled. In case of unforeseen events or illness, we’ll of course find an individual solution.

How does payment work?

You’ll receive an official invoice by email and, if you prefer, on WhatsApp. It lists all your lessons clearly. You can simply transfer the amount to the account stated.

Are there hidden costs, subscriptions or a contract commitment?

No! With me, there are no hidden costs, subscriptions or contract commitments. All prices and services are listed transparently. WhatsApp support and quick questions between lessons don’t cost anything extra either.

Can lessons also be in German?

Yes, of course. I teach in both English and German.

Do you have experience with ADHD, dyscalculia or learning difficulties?

Yes, I have a lot of experience with different learning difficulties, especially ADHD and dyscalculia. I adapt the pace, the explanations and the structure of the lessons individually. What matters to me is finding a way of learning together that works for you.

What’s the difference between Standard and Premium?

Both packages include individual 1:1 lessons and WhatsApp support between lessons.

With Premium, I also create personalised worksheets with review questions and practice tests that are tailored exactly to your level. This way, we can check and strengthen your knowledge, spot possible weak points and work on them specifically, so you’re well prepared for exams. I correct your answers and give you detailed feedback.

You can also send me your own exercises, homework or tests you’ve already written for correction at any time. You’ll get detailed feedback, and if needed, we’ll go through the tricky parts together in the next lesson.
